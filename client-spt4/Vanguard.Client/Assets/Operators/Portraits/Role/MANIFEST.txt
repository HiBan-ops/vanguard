Vanguard Operator static role/faction portrait set

Contract:
- JPEG only.
- 640 x 800 pixels.
- Each image must remain strictly below 100,000 bytes.
- The gameplay role is the primary visual identity; BEAR/USEC is a secondary faction influence.
- One BEAR and one USEC static portrait currently exist for each supported role:
  Assault, Recon, Support, Veteran, Marksman, Breacher, Medic.
- Runtime selection is deterministic from stable Operator identity within the matching role/faction pool.
- The pool-based resolver is intentionally compatible with later additional variants and the planned
  Vanguard semi-procedural portrait generator without making static media an authority over Operator data.
