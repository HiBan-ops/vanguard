Core source contracts:
- Persistent Operator world identity is independent of transient contract offers and ActiveService membership.
- Cumulative migration is driven by persisted schema and markers; unsupported states fail closed.
- Main-menu squad presentation is read-only and does not create a second gameplay authority.
- Standalone, Fika Host and Fika Headless consume the same persistent Operator identity and data contracts.
- Off-Raid Operator sessions have explicit entry and exit boundaries and preserve player-profile isolation.
- SAIN owns combat movement and individual combat decisions; Vanguard arbitrates compatible squad-level capabilities.
- Tactical Authoring automatically admits available HOLD slots on owner entry into a saved zone, and releases them on exit.
- Grenade safety, medical care and admissible looting may interrupt out-of-combat positioning under their own rules.
- Loot excursions can restore the applicable HOLD when the owner remains in the zone; otherwise Cohesion resumes.
